━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Each tier now has a unique animated border effect on character cards.

  S — Animated rainbow border and badge. All colors cycle continuously
      around the card edge. The S badge also rotates through the rainbow
      with a black outline keeping the text readable.

  A — Gold shimmer border. A bright gold highlight sweeps around the
      card border, like light reflecting off metal.

  B — Silver shimmer border. Same sweep effect in silver tones.
      B tier color updated from green to silver.

  C — Copper shimmer border. Same sweep effect in copper tones.
      C tier color updated from blue to copper.

  D — Plain gray border. No effect — intentionally understated.

The tier picker in the character detail screen reflects the updated
colors for B and C tiers.
